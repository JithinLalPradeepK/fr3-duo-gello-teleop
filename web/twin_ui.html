Scan the surroundings

Both arms rise from HOME and look around with their wrist cameras (about 1 minute of motion), then return home. Afterwards the wrist cameras are calibrated against the head camera and their close-range data is fused into the 3D scene.
For sharp, realistic objects the wrist cameras need their mounts measured once: print the calibration board (A4, 100 %), lay it flat about 60 cm in front of the robot, centred between the grippers, then scan. After that every scan fuses the close-range wrist data. Remove the board afterwards and scan again.
